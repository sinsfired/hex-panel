Hex Panel Sequencer

47 tiles mapped from the photo: 16 red, 15 green, 16 blue. Every sequence starts at the right edge and moves left.

Coordinate system

Col counts from the right edge in half-tile steps (1 to 29), so lower numbers light first. Row runs top to bottom (1 to 7). Rows are staggered: a tile and its neighbor in the row above differ by 1 column. q, r is the axial hex coordinate, handy for neighbor math.

Tile # is the panel numbering: red 1 to 16 from the right, then green 17 to 31, then blue 32 to 47, each group running right to left (top first when two tiles share a column). Each tile shows its number and its in-color ID (R1, G1, B1...). By color lights them in that order.

There is no single unbroken chain through all 47 tiles (the layout branches), so a strict wiring order can't be read off the shape alone. The three orders here are the useful ones: column sweep (a straight right-to-left wipe), one by one (same, a tile at a time), and connector wave (spreads tile-to-tile from the rightmost tile, the way a signal would travel through the edge connectors).
